# LinkedIn writing guide

The brand profile is authoritative. Use these defaults only when the profile does not override them.

## Structure

A strong default post has:
1. an immediate, specific hook;
2. a short setup that explains why the topic matters;
3. useful substance in compact paragraphs or bullets;
4. one clear takeaway;
5. a restrained CTA when appropriate.

## Style

- Write like a knowledgeable practitioner, not a generic marketing bot.
- Prefer concrete observations, examples, numbers, or contrasts over vague claims.
- Keep paragraphs short enough for mobile reading.
- Avoid filler openings such as “In today’s fast-paced world”.
- Avoid excessive emoji, hashtags, exclamation points, and rhetorical hype.
- Do not fabricate outcomes, testimonials, customer counts, or performance metrics.
- Preserve the user's voice when source material is provided.

## Carousel copy

- One idea per slide.
- Cover slide: short hook, not a paragraph.
- Internal slides: one headline plus one compact supporting thought.
- Final slide: takeaway or CTA, not both unless naturally concise.
- The LinkedIn post text should complement the carousel rather than repeat every slide verbatim.
